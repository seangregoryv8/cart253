## Description

This is a mod of the bestseller hit title: Frogfrogfrog

However, I added a few parts of my own.

### Story
> You play as Felix Vanderhold, a grave robber in search of something personal... the soul of your sister, Sophie Vanderhold.<br><br>Your sister was taken from you, an unfortunate victim of being at the wrong place at the wrong time. You knew it would happen, but weren't fast enough to prevent it. Now, tormented with the guilt, you ponder and stare upon the night sky, wondering if she's still there... looking over at you.<br><br>One day, the grief is too much, and you decide to study a mysterious form of black sorcery, enchanting it into a powerful net capable of capturing the souls of the damned.<br><br>But it all came at a price... a devilish figure demands a sacrifice, a certain amount of captured souls.<br><br>It is now your job to go out on your trusted boat in order to capture these souls, in hopes that you retrieve enough for the devils bidding. Whether he releases Sophie or not, whether you randomly find her in your journey, is unknown... All you do know is that you cannot suffer another sleepless night without the truth... without some form of closure.

### Gameplay
You play as Felix in this adventure, moving around in your boat to capture souls.

As you'd expect, the waters are rough, so you need to move accordingly to the waves, which sometimes drag you further than you'd like to.

Control the boat with A and D to move left and right respectively.

Launch the net with W

For each ghost you capture, you're rewarded points based on the ghost type, including how fast it was, how much it moved, and the frequency of its waves, alongside gifting you further points for a "head catch", or catching it with the head of the net, rather than catching it reeling back.

However, be careful, as any ghost you missed is calculated in the same way and deducted from your score.

If your score goes below 0, you lose the game.

### Strategy

Whilst a head catch is worth more, the net gets reeled back, not allowing you to move until it's fully reeled back.

However, you can also cast the net beforehand, allowing you to reel in possibly multiple ghosts if timed right during the reel in.

## Features

### Co-op
> Along Felix's journey is his lifelong friend, Charlie Reflio, who has been with him through everything ever since grade school. He became a fisherman, and crafted a boat specifically for Felix after hearing of Sophies passing and seeing him slowly begin to wade the waters with his net in search of souls.<br><br>Charlie decided to join in, initially rowing Felix around. However, Felix needed more help, and crafted a second net specifically for Charlie. Now, they both head out along the waters, in search of her.<br><br>Charlie, however, is a more laid back man, wanting to bring back Felix' better side again, and sometimes challenges him to friendly competitions in the water.

You can have a second person control Charlie in a co-op mode, where you both capture ghosts and see who comes out on top!

Charlie controls the same as Felix, but with J and L controlling the boats movement and I controlling his net.

### Difficulty
You can select between 3 difficulties, which change a lot of the gameplay around.

#### EASY
For the more laid back experience. Ghosts are slower, spawn slower, and are easier to predict, you need less points to win, you start off with more points, and any loss deducts less points from your score.

#### MEDIUM
Run of the mill difficulty, how the entire game was initially designed

#### HARD
For the true grieving brothers. Ghosts are faster, spawn faster, and are harder to predict, you need many more points to win, you have very few points to start off with, and any loss deducts more points from your score.

### Multiple Endings
You have 8 different endings in the game:
- 3 for singleplayer
- 5 for multiplayer

Can you get them all?

### Graphical overhaul
Many things here have been redesigned from the ground up, using only the p5js engine, with only one singular image being added later for an eerie effect (try to find it!).

The hunters have semi-realistic eyes, the boats are detailed with lanterns, the nets can have the captured ghosts inside of them as they get reeled in, and much more!

I heavily encourage people to check out all the little QOL improvements I added into this game.
