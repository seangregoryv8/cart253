Funny frog game ideas by Sean Gregory

1. The Frog is replaced with a ghostbuster (Goes with the theming, halloween edition, maybe a net to catch ghosts)
2. The Frog is the undertaker, reaping souls (Similar saying, but maybe with the frog moving to various places)
3. You play on a river (It dynamically changes the background, making it feel more alive)
4. The Bugs could move around in random orders (Offers a cool form of difficulty)
5. The bugs could have a difficulty slider, where an adjustable setting makes them go faster as it goes by (Offers an actual difficulty curve, as hard or easy as you want)
6. You could have bosses with more health (Offers a fun mid-game fight with something where you have to test your wits)
7. There could be a scoring system that depletes when you reach 0 (Offers a game-over system to incentivize doing well)
8. Everything contributes to the score system, with various things leading to a better score (Gives you a chance to actually showcase progress and brag to your friends about how much better you are)
9. The frog could have various small animations (Makes him feel more lively, whether we do different skins or not)
10. An intro screen with settings (Gives you a chance to throw yourself into the game instead of having the game do it for you)
11. Easter eggs for various settings chosen (Fun replayability to check out)
12. Different modes for the game being played (Gives you variety for when you get bored of the main game, but what kind of modes can I choose?)
13. Bug survival mode (Maybe some kind of three death mode, where it actually reduces points if you miss bugs and then calculates how many you killed)
14. Bug ping-pong (Bugs turn the ohter direction as you ping pong them from side to side with yourself, seeing if you can go for a high score)

16. Frog vs frog (basically co-op mode, where another control scheme allows for someone else to control a secondary frog. Or maybe even control the bug waves)
17. Optional fun physics (Basically whatever I feel like putting in as extras, maybe some sort of bouncing system, different flying methods for the things)
18. End screen to win (Maybe, for one of the modes, there's an actual win option, and it gives you a way to see an actual ending to Mr. Frog or Ghostbuster or undertaker or whatever else Im gonna make him)
19. Possibly some form of high score system (I'm talking an international system, where the local machine can actually remember your score and give you an end-score at the end to show how much better you are than someone else INTERNATIONALLY)
20. Controller compatability (Just a little way to add an extra method of playing to the game)
